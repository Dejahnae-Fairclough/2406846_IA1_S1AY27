"7 Cures for a Lean Purse"

This chapter focuses on simple ways to improve your financial situation. It explains the importance of saving part of your income, controlling spending, making smart investments, and protecting your money. It also highlights planning for future needs and finding ways to improve your skills and earn more over time.

"5 Laws of Gold"

The second chapter explains the Five Laws of Gold and how to manage money wisely. It shows the importance of saving regularly, making money grow, protecting what you have, avoiding investments you don’t understand, and being careful of offers that promise quick and easy money.

Personal Reflection & Biggest Takeaway

Reading The Richest Man in Babylon by George S. Clason taught me the importance of saving consistently, spending wisely, and planning for the future.

My biggest takeaway is that financial success comes from discipline and good money management. I can apply these lessons by budgeting my money, saving regularly, and making smarter financial decisions as a university student.
